FOUNDER

Built from firsthand experience.

Sonu Jangra
Founder, 48Projects
10+ Years 200+ Projects Pan-India

"I've always enjoyed visualizing a project before it exists — planning utilities, designing efficient layouts, coordinating execution, and watching an idea become a fully operational facility."

For more than a decade, Sonu has been involved in planning, managing, and delivering commercial laundry projects across India — contributing to 200+ projects across design, MEP coordination, utility planning, execution support and project management, across hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, railways, defence establishments, and industrial laundries.

Along the way, the same problem kept repeating: commercial laundries are highly specialized facilities, yet they're often treated like ordinary building services. Contractors experienced in general construction rarely understand the specific engineering, utility and workflow requirements a laundry needs — and that gap causes delays, rework and avoidable cost.

48Projects was founded to close that gap — not to become another contractor or consultancy, but to bring engineering, planning and execution under one point of ownership.

Sonu's project experience comes from over a decade working across the commercial laundry industry. 48Projects is his own independent venture — it is not affiliated with, and does not represent, any organization he has worked with.
